The Internet is not a direct pipe from browser to server.
  • 1DNS stays off the origin path: Your ISP forwards the lookup to a Recursive Resolver, which walks the Root (.) → TLD (.com) → Authoritative tree.
  • 2Physical hops dominate latency: TCP, TLS, and HTTP packets cross local gateways, Tier-1 BGP routers, and trans-Atlantic fiber before reaching Frankfurt.
  • 3Compare Cold vs Warm: Switch to Warm Keep-Alive to see how connection pooling skips DNS, TCP, and TLS handshakes completely.
